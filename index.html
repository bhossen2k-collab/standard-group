About Standard Group

A manufacturing partner that thinks like a design studio.

Standard Group is a full-package garment manufacturer built around one idea: buyers shouldn't have to choose between speed, quality and original development. We design, sample and produce Men's, Women's and Kids' apparel for brands and retailers ranging from fast-fashion volume players to premium contemporary labels.

Every product on this site is an own-development style — designed by our in-house team, specced to industry-standard tech packs, and ready to be adapted to your brand's fit block, fabric standards and labeling.
